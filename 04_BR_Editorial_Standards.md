# 04 — BR EDITORIAL STANDARDS
## Banana Republic Intelligence OS
### Version 4.0 | July 2026

---

## THE CONTENT MODEL

Every post is built around:

**EVENT → CONTRADICTION → MECHANISM → EVIDENCE → IMPLICATION → SIGNAL**

These labels are never published. They are the internal construction system.

---

## THE POST SPINE

Every BR post passes through these five stages internally:

**1. INTERRUPT** — Create a reason to stop. Contradiction. Curiosity gap. Puzzle.
**2. ORIENT** — Just enough context. One or two sentences. Entry point only.
**3. REVEAL** — Show the mechanism. The thing the headline did not name.
**4. CONNECT** — Why the mechanism matters beyond this one event.
**5. OPEN** — One specific condition to watch or one unresolved implication.

---

## THE SEVEN STORY FORMS

The OS chooses automatically based on the mechanism.

---

### STORY FORM 1 — CONTRADICTION
**Use for:** Unexpected price reactions, good news/bad reaction, bad news/good reaction
**Structure:** Open with the contradiction → explain what was priced in → reveal the mechanism → give the signal

Example opening type:
> A stock can deliver the quarter investors wanted and still lose 14% because the quarter was already priced in.

---

### STORY FORM 2 — CAUSAL CHAIN
**Use for:** Multi-step market structure, domino effects across assets
**Structure:** Start at the trigger → show each chain link → land at the investor's portfolio or decision

Example opening type:
> The move in Treasury yields this week wasn't about inflation. It was about who had to buy.

---

### STORY FORM 3 — BEFORE / AFTER
**Use for:** Expectation changes, positioning shifts, repricing events
**Structure:** What the market believed → what changed → what the new belief means for price

Example opening type:
> Three weeks ago the market was pricing three rate cuts. This week it's pricing one. Here's what shifted.

---

### STORY FORM 4 — CROSS-ASSET
**Use for:** Same mechanism appearing across stocks, crypto, rates, commodities
**Structure:** Show multiple assets → reveal the shared mechanism → explain why they all moved together

Example opening type:
> Bitcoin, gold, and small-cap stocks all rallied on the same day. They usually don't agree. When they do, it's worth understanding what they're agreeing about.

---

### STORY FORM 5 — MARKET MEMORY
**Use for:** Current event matching a historical BR mechanism
**Structure:** Today's event → the historical parallel → the common mechanism → what was different → the signal

Example opening type:
> We've seen this setup before.

---

### STORY FORM 6 — DEVELOPING SIGNAL
**Use for:** Events where the outcome is not yet resolved
**Structure:** What's happening → why it's unresolved → what evidence would confirm or deny → what to watch

---

### STORY FORM 7 — VISUAL EXPLAINER
**Use for:** Mechanisms better expressed through diagram, chart, or silent video
**Structure:** Designed for the visual first — caption accompanies the visual

## POST LENGTH — NEW STANDARD

**Core feed post:** 70-130 words
**Strong single-mechanism post:** 50-90 words
**Complex causal explanation:** 100-160 words
**Short observation:** 25-60 words

The correct length is whatever is required to make the mechanism understandable without padding.

Minimum words. Maximum understanding.

Do not compress important causal reasoning to hit a low count.
Do not expand a simple mechanism to appear more analytical.

---

## THE PRIORITY SCORE

Score every candidate before writing. Do not manufacture scores.

| Dimension | Score | Question |
|---|---|---|
| Causal Clarity | 1-5 | Can we explain why it moved with evidence? |
| Contradiction | 1-5 | Is there a meaningful gap between headline and reaction? |
| Investor Usefulness | 1-5 | Can the reader use this framework again? |
| Distribution Potential | 1-5 | Does the story create curiosity, discussion, or visual interest? |
| Timeliness | 1-5 | Is this relevant now? |

**18+ = Strong candidate**
**21+ = Priority**
**24-25 = Exceptional**

Below 18 = HOLD or REJECT

## THE VISUAL RECOMMENDATION SYSTEM

Every selected post receives a visual recommendation:

**TEXT ONLY** — mechanism stronger without visual
**DIAGRAM** — causal flow between entities
**CHART + ANNOTATION** — price, yields, or flows are essential evidence
**EVIDENCE SCREENSHOT** — the actual filing, headline, or disclosure is part of the proof
**SILENT VIDEO** — mechanism best expressed as a visual sequence

### Silent Video Standard
The viewer must understand the story with sound off.

Video structure:
```
SCENE 1 — HOOK: The contradiction or curiosity gap
SCENE 2 — CONTEXT: What happened / what everyone saw
SCENE 3 — CONTRADICTION: Why the obvious reading is incomplete
SCENE 4 — MECHANISM: What actually caused the move
SCENE 5 — CONSEQUENCE: What this means for the investor
SCENE 6 — SIGNAL: What to watch next
```

Visual elements to use: price movement, numbers, headlines, charts, arrows, highlighted text, filings, timelines, simple diagrams, company logos, market data, transitions.

No presenter required. No talking head. No generic stock footage. No slideshow of headlines. No loud meme editing.

## THE POSTING RHYTHM

The market determines content. No fixed daily schedule.

**Quiet day:** 1-2 strong posts
**Normal day:** 2-4 strong posts
**High-information day:** 3-5 strong posts
**Major event day:** 5+ only when each post reveals a distinct mechanism

Never create weak posts to hit a number.

---

## WHAT EARNS A POST

Post when:
- Price reaction contradicts the headline
- Expectations explain more than the headline
- Positioning mattered more than the news
- Capital flows explain the move
- Institutional behavior explains what retail misread
- A mechanical event created price pressure
- Macro data affected markets through a second-order mechanism
- Several assets moved for the same underlying reason
- The market moved before the event
- Good news produced a bad reaction or bad news produced a good reaction
- A familiar market pattern appeared again

**Do not post when:**
- A stock moved without mechanism insight
- A headline is trending but everyone already said what BR would say
- The event is merely news with no mechanism lesson
- BR already explained the same mechanism today without new evidence

---

## THE EVENT STATE TRACKING

**WATCHING** — Potential story, insufficient evidence
**DEVELOPING** — Evidence accumulating, not ready
**POSTED** — BR explained this mechanism
**UPDATED** — Material change after publication — eligible for follow-up
**CLOSED** — No longer developing

A repeated headline without new evidence is not a new post.
Only UPDATED stories return as follow-ups.

---

## THE FINAL RESEARCH FILTER

Before approving any post, confirm all of these:

- [ ] **FACTUAL** — Are all claims verified from primary sources?
- [ ] **CAUSAL** — Is the mechanism supported by evidence, not assumed?
- [ ] **DIFFERENTIATED** — Does BR explain something the headline does not?
- [ ] **USEFUL** — Does the reader learn something reusable beyond this event?
- [ ] **CURRENT** — Is this still relevant now?
- [ ] **HUMAN** — Does it sound like a person, not a template?
- [ ] **CONCISE** — Can anything be removed without losing the mechanism?
- [ ] **DISTRIBUTABLE** — Would someone naturally reply, quote, repost, or share this?
- [ ] **VISUAL** — Would a diagram or silent video make the mechanism clearer?
- [ ] **NON-REPETITIVE** — Has BR already said essentially this today?

If any critical category fails — revise or reject.

---

## NUMBERS RULE

Always digits. Never words.
90% not "ninety percent." 11bps not "eleven basis points." $216M not "two hundred sixteen million." 3-5x not "three to five times."

If a number would open a sentence — restructure.
