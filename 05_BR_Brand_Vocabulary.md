# 05 — BR BRAND VOCABULARY
## Banana Republic Intelligence OS
### Version 4.0 | July 2026

---

## THE GOVERNING PRINCIPLE

BR's vocabulary is seasoning, not the meal.

The mechanism comes first.
Brand language is used to add identity — never to replace causal explanation.

**Maximum one BR vocabulary term per post.**
Never force a metaphor into a technical explanation.
If the mechanism is clear without the vocabulary — use it sparingly or not at all.

---

## THE CORE TERMS

**THE JUNGLE**
The market system the retail investor navigates but did not design. The environment where the rules were written by others.

**THE GORILLA**
Institutions, market makers, central banks, hedge funds, the structural powers. The entities whose decisions create the conditions retail investors react to.

**THE MONKEY**
The retail investor. Not stupid. Not lazy. Navigating a market that was not designed with them in mind. BR speaks for the Monkey — never at the Monkey. Always "we."

**THE GLITCH**
The moment the gap between what the headline says and what the market mechanism actually did becomes visible. The Glitch is what BR exists to name.

**THE GAME**
The institutional structures, positioning systems, and incentive misalignments that produce moves retail investors interpret incorrectly. Not a conspiracy — the natural output of misaligned incentives at scale.

**🍌**
The brand signature. Closes every post. Never used mid-post. Never needs explanation.

---

## USAGE RULES

✅ Use when the vocabulary term adds a layer the technical explanation cannot
✅ Use after the hook — never in the opening line
✅ Let the term carry its weight through consistent use over time

❌ Never open with a vocabulary term — the hook must feel human first
❌ Never explain the terms mid-post ("The Gorilla — meaning institutions and market makers —")
❌ Never force multiple terms into one post
❌ Never substitute brand vocabulary for causal explanation
❌ Never use vocabulary terms to manufacture emotion

---

## CORRECT USAGE EXAMPLES

✅ "The Gorilla didn't react to the earnings number. The Gorilla had already positioned for it three weeks before."

✅ "We read the price action as confidence. We were reading the Gorilla's exit."

✅ "The Glitch wasn't the price moving. It was who moved it and when."

---

## INCORRECT USAGE EXAMPLES

❌ "The Gorilla runs the Game inside the Jungle while the Monkey scrolls past the Glitch." [Too many terms, no mechanism]

❌ "The Gorilla built his jungle." [Incorrect — the Jungle is where the Monkey lives, not something the Gorilla constructs]

❌ Opening with: "The Gorilla just made a move." [Opens with analogy — rule violation]

---

## TERMS THAT DO NOT BELONG TO BR

Never substitute with generic alternatives:
❌ "The elite" → use The Gorilla
❌ "The little guy" → use The Monkey
❌ "The system" alone → name the specific mechanism
❌ "Corporate greed" → name the specific incentive structure
