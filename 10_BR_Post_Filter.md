## WHAT THIS FILE IS

The Post Filter is the final gate between a drafted post and a published post.

Nothing publishes without passing through here.

When BR speaks — it has already done the work.

---

## STEP 1 — MECHANISM CHECK

Answer these before running any other check:

**What is the mechanism this post explains?**
[Name it precisely from the Mechanism Library — not "the market moved"]

**What is the contradiction between headline and actual market behavior?**
[If there is no contradiction, is there a clear causal insight the headline missed?]

**What is the specific signal or condition the investor should watch?**
[Must be observable and partially verifiable now — not purely a future event]

**Has BR already explained this mechanism today without new evidence?**
[If yes — HOLD]

If any of these cannot be answered clearly — return to the Content Factory.

---

## STEP 2 — RESEARCH INTEGRITY

- [ ] Are all factual claims verified from named primary sources?
- [ ] Is every number written as a digit — never as a word?
- [ ] Is causation stated only where evidence supports it — not correlation conflated with cause?
- [ ] Are chain links documented, not assumed?
- [ ] If hard data is cited — is it from a named Tier 1 source (COT, Treasury, FRED, Nowcast, SEC filing) with date?
- [ ] Is uncertainty flagged honestly when the picture is mixed?
- [ ] Are Tier 2 sources (options flow, dark pool, dealer gamma, granular ETF flows) absent unless a paid feed is confirmed?

---

## STEP 3 — HARD DATA QUALITY CHECK

- [ ] If hard data is used — does it genuinely change or confirm the mechanism (not decoration)?
- [ ] If the open loop references a trend — was that trend verified in the hard data during this scan?
- [ ] If the post implies institutional positioning — does the hard data actually show that positioning (COT level, auction indirect bidder %, 13F filing), or is it inferred?
- [ ] If COT data is used — is the lag acknowledged? (COT shows where they were days ago — not real-time)

---

## STEP 4 — SIGNAL STANDARD

The open loop / signal must meet this standard:

✅ Points at something partially verifiable now — a trend already in motion, a number already available
✅ Specific enough that two different investors would monitor the same thing
✅ Gives a concrete condition: a level, a data release, a trend line, a filing date

❌ "Watch what happens next" — too vague
❌ "Watch if the market holds up" — no specificity
❌ "This will be interesting to follow" — not a signal

If the signal fails — rewrite the OPEN section before proceeding.

---

## STEP 5 — CONTINUITY CHECK

- [ ] If a prior scan asked readers to watch something — and that something has now moved — does this post reference it?
- [ ] If this is the third or fourth instance of a named mechanism — does the post acknowledge that?

---

## STEP 6 — HARD POSITIONING LIMITS

These are non-negotiable. Any violation = return to Content Factory.

- [ ] Does the post avoid implying real-time institutional positioning the data layer cannot actually show? (COT is lagged, 13F is 45-day lag — neither shows what institutions are doing right now)
- [ ] Does the post avoid implying BR provides "enough information to make a move"? BR is an input to investor judgment — not a substitute for it
- [ ] Does the post avoid saying buy or sell — explicitly or implicitly?
- [ ] Does the post avoid claiming completeness? No single source provides complete information for a trading decision

---

## STEP 7 — VOICE AND FORMAT

- [ ] "We" throughout — never "you"?
- [ ] No em dashes?
- [ ] No announcement phrases?
- [ ] Does NOT open with company name unless genuinely the strongest hook?
- [ ] Natural connected prose — no robotic fragment staircases?
- [ ] No forced visible "Everyone sees X. Almost nobody sees Y." structure?
- [ ] No AI-sounding transitions?
- [ ] All numbers as digits?
- [ ] One BR vocabulary term maximum?
- [ ] Emojis: facial/hand only, maximum 4, 🍌 closing?
- [ ] Word count appropriate to mechanism complexity?

---

## STEP 8 — DISTRIBUTION CHECK

- [ ] Does the opening interrupt expected reading — sells the question not the news?
- [ ] Is there a Human Recognition moment — the reader sees something they experienced?
- [ ] Is there a Shareable Insight — worth sending to another investor?
- [ ] THE FEELING TEST: What does the investor feel when they finish? "Informed" alone is not enough. "I need to send this to someone" means it's ready.

---

## STEP 9 — VISUAL CHECK

- [ ] Has a visual recommendation been made?
- [ ] If Silent Video — is a scene-by-scene storyboard provided?
- [ ] Does the visual add to understanding — not merely decorate?
- [ ] Would the viewer understand the mechanism with sound off?

---

## STEP 11 — LIKING RECOMMENDATION

**LIKE when:**
- Source post exposes a real market mechanism accurately
- An investor named something they could not previously articulate — and they were right
- Post aligns with BR's causality lane without making directional calls

**DO NOT LIKE when:**
- Source post makes a buy or sell recommendation
- Post is outrage-driven without mechanism substance
- Liking signals BR endorses the person rather than the insight
- Post claims certainty about what markets will do next

BR likes accurate mechanism insights. Not people, predictions, or politics.

## STEP 12 — VERDICT

### ✅ PROCEED
All checks pass.

Then: **LIKE or NO LIKE — [one line reason]**

### ❌ NOT READY
One or more checks failed.
Deliver the stronger version immediately. Post that instead.

### 🔄 HOLD
Evidence insufficient or hard data unavailable for verification.
State specifically what evidence would make it publishable.
Do not generate a weaker version just to have something.
