That's more interesting than simply saying yields are up or down. One institution is changing the expected price of money. Another is actively intervening in the market for its own debt.

Watch the spread between the 2-year and 10-year into the afternoon. If the 2-year keeps climbing while the 10-year stays anchored, the curve isn't disagreeing with itself. It's showing us two institutions pulling in opposite directions. 🍌
