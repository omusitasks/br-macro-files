## THE GOVERNING PRINCIPLE

Distribution in 2026 runs through the feeling brain, not the thinking brain.

Posts that make people understand something build trust slowly.
Posts that make people feel something immediately trigger reaction, sharing, and engagement.

But BR does not manufacture anger. BR creates recognition.

The ideal reader response is:

> "This is what I was trying to explain."

Not: "This made me angry."

---

## THE NEW DISTRIBUTION ARCHITECTURE

Replace Accusation Line / Anger Anchor / Shareable Verdict with:

### HUMAN RECOGNITION
Make the reader recognize something they have experienced or witnessed in the market.

> "We see the stock fall and assume the business disappointed. Sometimes the market was simply unwinding a position."

This creates loyalty. The reader feels understood, not manipulated.

### SHAREABLE INSIGHT
Give the reader one sentence worth sending to another investor because it explains something they could not previously articulate.

The test: would an investor screenshot this to send to someone in their group chat?

### OPEN LOOP
End with one legitimate unresolved implication or a specific observable condition.

Not rhetorical bait. A genuine mechanism or signal still in motion.

## THE FIVE EMOTIONAL ENGINES

Use one per post. Declare before writing. The engine shapes the framing, not the facts.

**FEAR**
Core feeling: Something is happening to our money and we were not ready.
Share trigger: People forward fear to warn someone they care about.
Use for: Coming market pressure, structural risks, timing mechanisms.

**PRIDE**
Core feeling: We now see something most investors missed.
Share trigger: People share to signal they are ahead of the crowd.
Use for: Information asymmetry reveals, mechanism explanations, historical parallels.

**SHAME (EXPOSURE)**
Core feeling: We made a move without understanding what we were actually responding to.
Share trigger: People share to process the exposure and warn others.
Use for: Retail behavior misreads, basis trade unwinds, narrative cycles.

**RECOGNITION (REPLACES RAGE)**
Core feeling: This is exactly the mechanism I sensed but could not name.
Share trigger: People share to say "this is what I was trying to explain."
Use for: Contradiction stories, cross-asset connections, market structure reveals.

**HOPE (EQUIPPED)**
Core feeling: Knowing this changes how we will read the next move.
Share trigger: People share to appear helpful and wise to other investors.
Use for: Monitoring conditions, signal posts, developing story updates.

## EMOTIONAL ENGINE SELECTION GUIDE

| Story Type | Primary Engine |
|---|---|
| Good news / bad reaction | Recognition or Shame |
| Bad news / good reaction | Recognition or Pride |
| Mechanism retail always misreads | Shame |
| Coming market pressure | Fear |
| Historical pattern repeating | Recognition or Fear |
| Institutional behavior exposed | Recognition |
| Information advantage revealed | Pride |
| Monitoring condition / signal | Hope |
| Cross-asset connection | Pride or Recognition |

## THE POST SPINE — INTERRUPT / ORIENT / REVEAL / CONNECT / OPEN

Every BR post passes through this internal sequence. The labels never appear publicly.

**1. INTERRUPT**
Create a reason to stop. A contradiction. A curiosity gap. A puzzle.
The reader should think: "Wait. How does that work?"

**2. ORIENT**
Give just enough context to understand what happened. One or two sentences. Entry point only.

**3. REVEAL**
Show the mechanism. This is the BR value. The thing the headline did not name.

**4. CONNECT**
Explain why the mechanism matters beyond this one event. Make it reusable.

**5. OPEN**
Give one specific thing to watch or one unresolved implication. Concrete and observable.

Before writing any post, score the candidate (Research Protocol handles this).

High-scoring posts earn writing time. Low-scoring posts get held or rejected.

Emotional engines amplify strong mechanisms. They do not rescue weak ones.

## X DISTRIBUTION PRINCIPLES

X's recommendation systems reward: replies, reposts, quotes, clicks, follows, media interaction, video views, dwell time, and authentic original content.

BR should optimize for:

**STOP** — Strong contradiction or curiosity gap in the first sentence
**STAY** — Clear explanation that rewards continued reading or viewing
**UNDERSTAND** — A mechanism that becomes obvious by the end
**RESPOND** — A question, implication, or disagreement worth discussing
**SHARE** — A useful explanation people want others to see
**RETURN** — A recurring mechanism readers associate with BR over time

Do not use engagement bait.
Do not ask empty questions to generate replies.
Do not manufacture controversy.
Do not chase the algorithm — design content that naturally earns meaningful interaction.

---

## THE NODDING TEST

If a reader can finish a post, nod, and scroll on — the post failed.

Nodding is a thinking-brain response. Sharing is a feeling-brain response.

Before publishing, ask: What does the reader feel when they finish this?

If the answer is "informed" — the post may still fail distribution.
If the answer is "I need to send this to someone" — the post is ready.

---

## THE VISUAL DISTRIBUTION LAYER

High-value posts should also consider whether the mechanism is better expressed visually.

Visual content that explains a mechanism silently — without a presenter, without talking heads — can dramatically increase stop rate, dwell time, and shares.

The OS recommends a visual type for every post and provides a storyboard when video is selected.

Visual types:
- **TEXT ONLY** — mechanism stronger without visual support
- **DIAGRAM** — causal flow between entities
- **CHART + ANNOTATION** — when price, yields, or flows are essential evidence
- **EVIDENCE SCREENSHOT** — when the actual filing, headline, or disclosure is part of the proof
- **SILENT VIDEO** — when the mechanism is best explained as a visual sequence

The visual must tell the story, not merely accompany the caption.
