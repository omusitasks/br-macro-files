#######POST 6 midday

Brent is climbing. WTI is barely moving.

They're both oil, but right now they're pricing two different risks.

WTI is sitting near $86.81, little changed on the day. Brent is holding around $93–94 and heading for its second straight weekly gain, with Bessent saying details of the new Iran sanctions will come Monday.

It's a premium attached to a specific place.

Monday may tell us whether that premium spreads. If the sanctions are severe enough to affect supply beyond the immediate region, watch whether WTI starts closing the gap with Brent. That's when a chokepoint story starts becoming a global supply story. 🍌

That's worth remembering because the first explanation can be right until the next piece of information changes the market's mind.

Watch whether the Russell keeps the gain into the close. If it does, today's turnaround happened without the help from yields we thought it needed this morning. 🍌
