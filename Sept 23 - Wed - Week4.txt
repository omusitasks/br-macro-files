That matters because the split is no longer just a one-session reaction. 

Gold is still responding more directly to the changing uncertainty and rate backdrop, while Bitcoin is being pulled by liquidity, positioning and risk appetite.

The longer that divergence holds, the harder it becomes to explain both assets through the same macro variable. 

Today has three tests:

→ Does the 10-year finally follow oil lower, or does duration only respond to the barrel in one direction?

→ Do Barr at 10:05 and Jefferson at 10:20 stay focused on housing and Treasury-market functioning, or does either move into the rate path?

→ Does $XLF finally catch the relief, or does financial weakness remain isolated while semis and broader equities climb?

The value in this morning's move is not simply that oil is cheaper.

It is that cheaper oil is being asked to prove what it can actually repair.

→ Oil moved first.

→ Bonds, financials, gold and Bitcoin are still deciding what that move means. 🍌
