#######(TUESDAY - AUGUST 18TH 2026)

#######POST 1 morning

Yesterday we asked whether the 10-year would follow the 30-year higher.

This morning, it's getting close.

The 10-year is around 4.72%, with 4.75% still sitting in front of it. The 30-year has pushed to 5.31%, its highest level since June 2007.

For the first time since Friday, both ends are moving together.

Yesterday, the pressure was concentrated at the long end. Today, the 10-year is starting to feel it too.

Oil isn't helping. Brent is near $91 after Trump rejected an extension of the Iran ceasefire and threatened action over the Strait of Hormuz.

4.75% is still the number. If it breaks today, yesterday's split becomes a much bigger story.


#######POST 2 midday

Reddit gave us an interesting clue before today's index buying even began.

Monday's inclusion pop didn't hold. The stock opened at $177.51, reached $178.33, then spent the rest of the session falling to $164.50.... a roughly 7.6% drop.

That happened before today's roughly $3 billion of forced index buying arrived.

JPMorgan had already estimated the order at 16.7 million shares, nearly three times Reddit's normal daily volume.

So today's move needs to be read carefully.

If Reddit rises because billions of dollars of index demand have to find the stock, that tells us very little about what investors think Reddit is worth.

The interesting price comes after the forced buyer has finished.



#######POST 3 midday

Last week, oil and rising yields pushed Bitcoin down with stocks.

This morning, the same pressure is back... but Bitcoin isn't following.

Oil is above $90, Treasury yields remain elevated, and the major US indexes are red.

Bitcoin is around $64,000, up over the past 24 hours after briefly falling below $63,000.

Gold is moving with it.

The difference may be sitting underneath the price: spot Bitcoin ETFs have taken in more than 14,000 BTC over five days, reversing the outflows that dominated the previous stretch.

Same macro pressure. Different flow.

If Bitcoin keeps holding while oil and yields stay elevated, the market may be telling us the buyer underneath it has changed.



#######POST 4 afternoon

The number we were waiting for finally broke.

The 10-year crossed 4.75% this afternoon, reaching 4.77%. The 30-year moved to 5.34%, its highest level since 2007.

Yesterday, the long end was moving alone.

Today, the 10-year joined it.

The interesting part is what's not moving with them: the 2-year is barely changed, which means expectations for the Fed haven't shifted nearly as much as the cost of borrowing further out.

That makes today's move different from yesterday's.

The split we were watching has closed. Now we find out whether it stays closed.


#######POST 5 afternoon

This morning we asked whether Bitcoin could keep ignoring the same pressure that pushed it lower last week.

So far, it is.

The 10-year just broke 4.75%. The S&P, Dow and Nasdaq are extending their losses. Oil remains above $90.

Bitcoin is still around $64,500–65,000.

Last week, the same combination sent Bitcoin lower with stocks.

This time, something underneath the market appears to be absorbing the pressure: spot Bitcoin ETFs have taken in more than 14,000 BTC over five sessions.

That's a meaningful difference.

The question isn't whether Bitcoin can stay green for one afternoon.

It's whether the buyer underneath it is strong enough to keep absorbing the pressure if yields stay above 4.75%.


#######MARKET CLOSE/CENSUS POST

MARKET STRUCTURE CENSUS — AUG. 18, 2026

For three weeks, 4.75% kept holding on the 10-year Treasury.

Today it finally broke, touching 4.77% before settling back around 4.74%.

The 30-year went further, reaching 5.33% intraday — its highest level since 2007 — before closing around 5.28%.

That changes what we were watching yesterday.

The long end is no longer moving alone. The 10-year finally joined it, while the 2-year remained relatively calm. The market still sees a softer Fed path ahead, but investors are demanding more to lend money further out.

Oil didn't make that easier. Brent remained around $91 as the Iran situation stayed unresolved.

Stocks felt the pressure. Bitcoin didn't, holding around $64,500 despite the move in yields.

Yesterday we asked whether the 10-year would follow the 30-year.

Today, it did.

Now the question is whether the 2-year eventually follows them — or whether the long end keeps climbing while the Fed path stays calm. 🍌
