#######MARKET CLOSE/CENSUS POST

MARKET STRUCTURE CENSUS — SEPT. 17, 2026

The market finally stopped needing a reason to leave 5%.

That's the structural change.

The 10-year closed at 4.93%, down 7.6bps, with the 2-year and 30-year falling alongside it. 

For most of this month, every attempt to hold this level needed reinforcement: 
→ an oil shock, 
→ a record-demand Treasury auction, 
→ then a Fed press conference.

Today needed none.

That's different from simply saying yields fell.

A curve moving lower together without a fresh catalyst is the market giving back a premium that had become self-reinforcing. 

The question is whether that premium was ever as fundamental as the price action made it look.

The strongest evidence came from duration-sensitive equities.

Nasdaq 100 gained 1.73% against 1.12% for the S&P, with semiconductors up roughly 3%. 

The same chip sector that entered a confirmed bear market Monday on an AI-pacing headline rallied today without that debate changing.

The variable that changed was the discount rate.

But the repricing has a limit.

The VIX closed at 15.4, clearing the 16.34 level that had held for three sessions. 

Goldman also recovered almost the entire Wednesday shock, finishing essentially flat. 

Financials recovered with the broader sector rather than continuing to carry yesterday's company-specific concern.

Oil was the exception.

Brent remained around $104 and WTI around $100, still pricing Aramco's ship-to-ship workaround and the Energy Secretary's reassurance rather than a confirmed return of pipeline barrels.

And then there's the small-cap problem.

The SmallCap 600 gained only 0.06%.

→ If today's move were the beginning of a broad lower-cost-of-capital regime, small caps should be participating more aggressively.

Instead, the biggest response came from the long-duration large caps that had absorbed the most of the recent rate premium.

So today's move looks less like a new regime than a release of pressure where that pressure had accumulated.

Tomorrow gives us two tests.

→ Does 4.93% hold without another Treasury operation or Fed voice?

→ And does the Nasdaq-small-cap gap narrow?

→ If yields stay lower and breadth improves, today's relief is spreading.

→ If yields stay lower while large-cap duration keeps doing all the work, the market didn't change regimes.

It simply stopped paying yesterday's premium. 🍌
